How purchase and delivery work

1. Install the free extension
From the Chrome Web Store. It works in Chrome, Brave, Edge and other Chromium browsers.
2. Buy PRO
Click “Buy PRO” in the extension (Edit groups → Plan card). Payment is processed securely by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record, which also handles VAT and sales tax.
3. Receive your license key
Right after payment, the license key appears on the confirmation page and is emailed to you.
4. Activate
Paste the key in the Plan card and press Activate. PRO features unlock instantly. You can deactivate a device at any time to move the license to another one.

FAQ

Is this a subscription?
No. PRO is a single payment for a lifetime license.
Which payment methods are accepted?
Cards and the other methods offered by Lemon Squeezy at checkout (for example PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay, depending on your country).
Do you store my calendar data?
No. Your calendars and groups never leave your browser. See the privacy policy.
